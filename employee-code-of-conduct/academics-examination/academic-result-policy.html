OFFICE ORDER — Academic Result Policy: Fail Percentage, EIOP & Undertaking System

Established to bring uniformity and transparency to the academic result process across all six LMCS schools, discussed and agreed with all school Heads. Effective immediately.

Academic Result Policy

1. Definitions & Basic Parameters

These definitions apply uniformly across all schools of LMCS.

Applicable ClassesClass 3 and above. Does not apply to Montessori 1 through Class 2.
Board ClassAny class in which the student appears for an external Board examination (typically Class 10 and 12). All other Applicable Classes are Non-Board Classes.
Subject ScoreThe student's final score in a subject, as a percentage out of 100, combining theory and internal-assessment components.
Passing Percentage40%. At or above 40% is a Pass; below 40% is a Fail in that subject.
Grey Zone33% ≤ Subject Score < 40%. Eligible for the relief mechanisms in Section 3.
Outright FailSubject Score below 33%. Not eligible for Grace Marks — relief only via EIOP.
Theory ComponentThe written paper, distinct from internal assessment. Must independently be passed at 40% (32/80 for an 80-mark paper) — failing it fails the subject regardless of internal-assessment marks.
Internal AssessmentProject work, periodic tests, practicals, and other school-administered components.
EIOP (Eligible for Improvement of Performance)A re-examination opportunity for students who Fail a subject, under the conditions in Section 3. Same blueprint and syllabus as the final examination.
Grace MarksMarks added to a Grey Zone Subject Score to lift it to 40%, granted only per Section 3, with a written warning and Undertaking.
UndertakingA formal document signed by Parent/Guardian, Student and Principal granting Conditional Promotion. Two formats — Board and Non-Board — see Section 7 and the Annexure.
Conditional PromotionPromotion subject to a signed Undertaking. The student is on academic watch for the following academic year.

2. Scope & Application

Applies to all Applicable Classes (Class 3 and above) across all six LMCS schools. The Passing Percentage, Grey Zone and Outright Fail thresholds govern all subjects uniformly. There is no Principal-level discretion or waiver outside the relief mechanisms in Section 3.

3. Result Categories

3.1 Three or More Subjects Failed

Declared FAIL, not eligible for EIOP, must repeat the class.

3.2 Exactly 2 Subjects Failed

  • (a) Both Outright Fail (<33%): declared FAIL, must repeat the class. Not eligible for EIOP.
  • (b) One Outright Fail + one Grey Zone: EIOP required in both subjects. Outcome per Section 3.4.
  • (c) Both Grey Zone: EIOP only in the lower-scoring subject; the higher-scoring subject passes via Grace Marks (lifted to 40%). The lower subject's EIOP outcome follows Section 3.3(b) (treated as a one-EIOP case).

3.3 Exactly 1 Subject Failed

  • (a) Grey Zone (33%–40%): Grace Marks lift the score to 40%, subject passes, a warning is issued, and Conditional Promotion is granted once the Undertaking is signed (Section 7). No EIOP required.
  • (b) Outright Fail (<33%): EIOP required. Clears EIOP → promoted. Fails EIOP → may sign an Undertaking for Conditional Promotion; if declined, repeats the class.

3.4 Outcomes After EIOP

One EIOP taken: Section 3.3(b) applies. Two EIOPs taken:

  • (a) Fails both: declared FAIL, repeats the class. No Undertaking option.
  • (b) Passes 1 of 2: may sign an Undertaking for Conditional Promotion; if declined, repeats the class.
  • (c) Passes both: promoted to the next class.

4. EIOP Fee

₹200 per subject.

5. EIOP Examination Schedule

  • Must be conducted within 3 weeks of the declaration of the final result.
  • Blueprint and syllabus are the same as the final examination.

6. EIOP Preparation Period

  • Students get a minimum of 3 weeks to prepare.
  • EIOP students must not attend regular classes during this period.
  • May come to school for self-study, 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM, with separate seating arrangements.
  • Teachers available for guidance 2:00–3:00 PM, or during free periods.
  • Students may also prepare from home.
  • Must not purchase next-session books until EIOP is cleared.
  • After clearing EIOP, students independently cover the next class's syllabus.

7. Undertaking System

  • Used for Conditional Promotion under Section 3.3(a), 3.3(b), or 3.4(b). Two formats apply — see the Annexure below.
  • Signed by Parent/Guardian, Student, and Principal. Prepared by the School office within 7 days of the result or EIOP result.
  • Filed in the student's academic record. A student on Conditional Promotion is on academic watch for the following academic year.
  • Repeated Undertakings in consecutive years must be reported to the MD (Academics).

8. Parent Communication

  • All schools must clearly and promptly communicate EIOP dates, syllabus, and preparation guidelines to parents.
  • Parents should be advised not to purchase new book sets in advance.

Annexure — Undertaking Formats

Two formats apply, depending on whether the student is entering a Board Class or a Non-Board Class.

Format A — For Board Classes

I, ____________ (Student), studying in Class ______ at ____________ (School), along with my Parent/Guardian ____________, hereby acknowledge that I have been granted Conditional Promotion / EIOP relief under the Academic Result Policy.

I undertake that:

  1. I will appear for and pass all subjects in Term 1 of the Board Class.
  2. If I fail in even one subject in Term 1, I accept that I will not be eligible for inclusion in the List of Candidates (LOC) submitted to the Board, and I will repeat the Board Class.
  3. This undertaking is given voluntarily and is binding on me and my Parent/Guardian.

Signatures: Student, Parent/Guardian, Principal — each with date.

Format B — For Non-Board Classes

I, ____________ (Student), studying in Class ______ at ____________ (School), along with my Parent/Guardian ____________, hereby acknowledge that I have been granted Conditional Promotion / EIOP relief under the Academic Result Policy.

I undertake that:

  1. If I fail in 1 subject in the next academic year, I will be eligible for EIOP as per the prevailing policy.
  2. If I fail in 2 or more subjects in the next academic year, I accept that I will not be eligible for EIOP, and I will voluntarily repeat the class.
  3. This undertaking is given voluntarily and is binding on me and my Parent/Guardian.

Signatures: Student, Parent/Guardian, Principal — each with date.
