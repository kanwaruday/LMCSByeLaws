OFFICE ORDER — Trilingual Literary Fest (Master Document: Inter-House and Inter-LMS)

Supersedes the 2024 Literary Fest guidelines (OO/19/24) — see archived version.

Trilingual Literary Fest 2026

This document governs the Literary Fest across both stages of competition. Each school first conducts the Inter-House round among its four Houses. Students shortlisted there represent their school at the Inter-LMS final round, held among the four LMS schools. Event list, formats, themes and judgement parameters are identical at both stages — only the level of competition changes.

Teachers Responsible

  • Selection & preparation (Inter-House stage): House Moderator
  • Guidance (both stages): Language Teachers — English, Hindi and Sanskrit

Event Planning

  • Both the Inter-House and Inter-LMS stages are whole-day events; schools should plan accordingly.
  • Multiple events may be run simultaneously.
  • PPTs for the quiz rounds are prepared by the language teachers of the respective wing and school.
