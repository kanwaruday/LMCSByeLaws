OFFICE ORDER — "A Day at LMS" Video Editing Content Competition

A Day at LMS — Video Editing Competition

A video content competition conducted across all La Montessori branches for students of Classes 7 to 12, run entirely through individual entries. Fixed theme for every student across all classes and branches: "A Day at LMS."

Overview

Who Can ParticipateStudents of Classes 7 to 12 (individual entries only)
What Students DoBring a phone/camera to school on the competition day, record moments from their school day, edit into a short video, and upload to their class Google Drive folder within 3 days
Upload DeadlineEnd of school hours on Day 3 after the competition day
How Winners Are DecidedPublic voting via Facebook likes, shares and comments on each video
Applicable BranchesAll La Montessori Schools

Timeline & Responsibilities

WhenResponsibleDuty
3 days beforeClass TeacherAnnounce competition, collect participant names, share final list with Principal & IT Teacher
3 days beforeIT TeacherSet up class-wise Google Drive folders (Editor access for registered students & Class Teacher only), share the official high-resolution school logo (min. 300 DPI PNG)
1 day beforeClass TeacherFinalise participant list (no additions after this point); confirm folder/logo access with IT Teacher; remind class that only registered students may carry phones
Competition dayClass TeacherVerify devices at start of day; follow standard confiscation procedure for non-registered students carrying phones; brief students on recording rules and the Day 3 deadline
Day 1 & 2 afterStudentEdit footage at home; add title card, school logo, and closing card; prepare file (named ClassName_YourName, MP4 preferred, min. 720p)
Day 3 (deadline)Class TeacherCheck uploads against participant list; mark each student Uploaded / Not Uploaded on the TRR; confirm completion with IT Teacher in writing
After deadlineIT TeacherPost videos to the official school Facebook page, class-wise — all of one class's videos posted the same day before moving to the next class; tag each with name, class, section
While postedStudents / PublicVoting stays open (likes, shares, comments) until result compilation on 1st July
By 1st JulyIT Teacher / PrincipalTally engagement across all classes and finalise results

Video Requirements

  • Opening title card: name, class, branch, and "A Day at LMS"
  • School logo must appear on the title card or end card
  • Duration: Class 7 & 8 — 2 to 3 minutes; Class 9, 10 & 12 — 3 to 5 minutes
  • Closing card: "A Day at LMS" or a short personal message
  • File name: ClassName_YourName (no spaces) — e.g. 9A_RahulSharma
  • Format: MP4 preferred (MOV accepted), minimum 720p

Scoring — Public Engagement

Once posted to the official school Facebook page, each video is scored purely on public engagement:

  • Each Like = 1 point
  • Each Share = 2 points
  • Each Comment = 1 point

Engagement counts only on the official school Facebook page — likes, shares or comments on any other platform, or a personal repost, are not counted. Results are compiled and finalised by 1st July.

Rules for the Day

  • Only students on the registered list may carry phones on the competition day.
  • Any editing app may be used — CapCut, KineMaster, VN, iMovie, or others.
  • No recording of teachers or other students without their consent.
  • No copyrighted music — use royalty-free or original audio.
  • Devices are the student's own responsibility; the School is not liable for loss or damage.

Disqualification

  • Non-registered student brought a recording device to school
  • Use of copyrighted music, footage, or images without permission
  • Recording of staff or students without their consent
  • Content that is inappropriate, offensive, or contrary to school values
  • Missing school logo or title card in the submitted video
  • Upload after the Day 3 deadline without prior written approval from the Principal
  • Misuse of device on the competition day
  • Any attempt to artificially inflate likes, shares, or comments — bulk/bot activity or paid promotion
