D. Changes at School for Preparation

Zero Period for Preparation

Second tier added 2026-09-18 per OO-11/26 (the 2-hour escalation at 15 days); the original 1-hour/30-day rule is unchanged.

  • Starting 30 working days before the Annual Function, a daily Zero Period of 1 hour is allocated for preparation
  • Starting 15 working days before, the Zero Period extends to 2 hours
  • All classes prepare individually
  • Participation in the Annual Function is mandatory for all students

Participation Fee

Net-new 2026-09-18 per OO-11/26 — previously this page said only "students pay as per the information provided by School Management," with no published figures.

Per child (participating)₹900
Siblings₹700
Non-participants attending₹500
Non-participants not attending₹200 (cross-verified by Principals)

Classroom Setup

The classroom must be set up in a "U" formation to ensure effective practice areas during preparation.
