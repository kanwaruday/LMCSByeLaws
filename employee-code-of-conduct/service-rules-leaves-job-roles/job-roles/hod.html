OFFICE ORDER — The HOD System

Head of Department (HOD)

The HOD System gives teachers an opportunity to showcase leadership. Selected teachers take on mentoring roles, train fellow educators, and bring new ideas to improve learning — enhancing teaching quality and the overall learning experience across LMS.

8 HOD positions are open, each scoped to a subject/wing across the branches listed:

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct induction & training for new teachers (online & offline)
  • Support and handhold new and existing teachers, acting as experienced mentors
  • Improve academic systems — content, pedagogy, and school processes
  • Oversee department teachers and track their monthly performance
  • Guide department teachers on upcoming events and how to organise them
  • Ensure quality of Class Work Assessments (CWA)
  • Ensure quality of Term Exams and the Examination Process
  • Visit designated schools for staff feedback & training, when required

Selection Process (2-Step Evaluation)

  1. Application Form — interested teachers apply by the announced deadline.
  2. Discussion & Interview — a discussion on "Things I Want to Improve at LMS," followed by a final interview assessing leadership, vision, and system knowledge.
