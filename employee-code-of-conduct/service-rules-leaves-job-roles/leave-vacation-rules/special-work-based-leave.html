Special & Work-Based Leave

These leave categories are tied to school events, non-compliance, or special circumstances rather than a simple day-off request. Updated 2026-09-16 per OO-09/26 (Leave Norms Office Order).

Special Day Leave

Special Day Leave is applied when an employee is absent on a Special Day. It is a distinct category from Double Leave (the unrelated 10%-teacher-cap mechanism) — the two share a "2 days" figure but nothing else, and shouldn't be used interchangeably.

What counts as a Special Day:

  • Annual Functions
  • Inter-School Events
  • Open Houses / MOHs
  • MUN
  • Investiture Ceremony
  • Sports Day
  • First or last day of an academic term
  • Any other event where parents or external trainers are involved
  • Combined school meetings
  • Workshops
  • Graduation ceremonies, freshers, farewells

Special Day Absent Leave

The deduction depends on how much notice was given before the Special Day (updated 2026-09-16 — previously a flat 2-day/5-day split with no middle tier):

Notice Given Deduction
7 or more days' prior notice 2 leave days
Prior notice given, but less than 7 days 3 leave days
No notice given (Absent) 5 leave days

Non-Compliance Leave

Non-Compliance Leave is deducted when an employee fails to complete an assigned responsibility, submit a required document, or follow a school process by its deadline — even if the employee was physically present at school.

Rule Details
Deduction per instance −0.1 leave per non-compliance instance
Conversion 10 non-compliance instances = 1 full leave day deducted
Frequency Calculated and deducted monthly from the employee's leave balance
Examples of non-compliance Missing QP submission deadline, not updating CSM data or Online Portals on time, failing in daily reporting, failing to sign registers, not handing over duties before leave, failing to maintain school records
Non-Compliance deductions are automatically flagged by the System Coordinator and forwarded to the Admin Manager. Employees receive a monthly summary of any deductions recorded against them.
