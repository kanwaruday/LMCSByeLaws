General Leave Rules

The following rules apply to all employees across all LMCS branches, regardless of leave category. Updated 2026-09-16 per OO-09/26 (Leave Norms Office Order).

  1. Leave is authorised absence, not a right. Leave is a request, not a right — granting it is at the discretion of higher authorities. It must be applied for in advance through Google Spaces.
  2. 10% teacher cap. Not more than 10% of teaching staff at a branch may be on leave on a given day (rounded down). Teachers applying after the cap is reached for that day are marked Double Leave — see Day Leaves for the worked formula. Long Leave (more than 6 consecutive days) does not count toward this cap.
  3. First-come, first-served. Leave is granted in the order it is requested. No two staff members in the same role at the same branch may be on leave simultaneously without prior approval from MD Operations.
  4. Next-day absence: notify by 7:00 AM, via the Online Portal. Absence the next day must be notified no later than 7:00 AM that day, through the Online Portal. An absence not notified by this deadline is recorded as Absent, regardless of the reason.
  5. Responsibility handover is mandatory. Before proceeding on leave, the employee must hand over all pending duties, registers, and responsibilities to a designated colleague. Failure to do so may result in leave being treated as unauthorised.
  6. External duty vs. external remuneration. An employee remunerated by an external body for a working day is marked on leave from school that day. If not remunerated, the employee is on official duty and marked present.
  7. Open House. Not attending MOH/OH (Open House) is marked as Absent.
  8. Revocable. The Head of Institution may grant or revoke (revert) a leave.
  9. What counts as a half day. See Time-Based Leaves for the precise threshold (2⁄3 of the shift's working hours) — this replaces the older flat "2 hours' WOH absence" rule of thumb.
  10. Sanctioned leave does not guarantee pay. Whether leave is paid depends on the leave category (CL = paid; Unpaid Leave = unpaid). The employee must confirm the leave type with the Admin Manager before proceeding.
  11. Leave lapses on resignation. Any unused Casual Leave balance lapses on the date of resignation or termination. There is no encashment of CL at the time of leaving service.
  12. Probation is 1 year — unpaid vacation only. Employees with 1 year or less of service do not receive paid vacation; they otherwise accrue and use Casual Leave normally from their date of joining.
  13. Emergency leave. Death in the immediate family, under the same roof, is treated as emergency leave, with no leave deducted.
  14. Staff picnic. On a staff picnic day, no leave is marked; the employee contributes financially instead.
  15. Sandwiched leaves. If a leave is taken on a day immediately before or after a holiday (Sunday, gazetted holiday, or school vacation), the intervening holiday is also counted as leave unless specifically exempted by MD Operations.
  16. Leave records are maintained centrally. The Admin Manager maintains the master leave register. All branch Principals must report leaves by the end of each working day to ensure accurate records.
