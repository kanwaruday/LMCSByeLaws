Special Holiday Schedule for Staff

Section 8 of OO-09/26 (Leave Norms Office Order), updated 2026-09-16. Supersedes the 2023 schedule (OO/12/23) — see archived version.

Where an employee is expected to attend under this schedule but cannot, an application with supporting evidence is required. Coordinators are always on call.

Occasion Helper/Drivers Fee Clerks Teachers PTI/IT Principal
Vacations ½ Working Days, 9am–3pm ½ Working Days, 9am–3pm On Holiday ½ Working Days, 9am–3pm ½ Working Days, 9am–3pm
Gazetted / Local Holidays On Holiday On Holiday On Holiday On Holiday On Call
Addl. Gazetted Holiday (Women) On Holiday if after Fees days (Compensatory Leave if working) On Holiday if after Fees days (Compensatory Leave if working) On Holiday On Holiday On Call
Emergency Holiday Working 9am–1pm Working 9am–1pm Work from Home On Call On Call
Emergency Long Holiday Working 9am–1pm Working 9am–1pm Work from Home On Call On Call
Sandwich Holiday (school-given) On Holiday if after Fees days On Holiday if after Fees days Work from Home On Call On Call

On Call means you can be called to school if needed; no Compensatory Leave is granted for on-call status itself.
