Time-Based Leaves

Time-based leaves accumulate as fractional leave days based on the duration of partial absence. When sufficient instances accumulate, they convert into full leave deductions. Updated 2026-09-16 per OO-09/26 (Leave Norms Office Order).

Late Leave

Trigger: reaching school after the branch's "Late Leave Threshold Time" by up to 20 minutes. Reporting and threshold times differ by role:

Conversion rate4 Late Leaves = 1 full leave day deducted

Short Leave

Rule Details
Definition Absence from school of up to 60 minutes (updated 2026-09-16 — previously tracked proportionally per hour)
Conversion rate 3 Short Leaves = 1 full leave day deducted

Half Leave

A half day is defined as 2⁄3 of the branch's working hours for that shift length — not a literal half (updated 2026-09-16 per OO-09/26):

Shift LengthHalf Day Threshold
4-hour shift2 hr 40 min
6-hour shift4 hr
8-hour shift5 hr 20 min
RuleDetails
Conversion rate2 Half Leaves = 1 full leave day deducted
