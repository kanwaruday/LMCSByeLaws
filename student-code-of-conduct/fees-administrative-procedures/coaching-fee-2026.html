OFFICE ORDER — Coaching Fee Structure

Coaching Fee Structure and Payment Plan for Academic Session 2026–27, applicable to Coaching Classes at LMS Kelheli.

Coaching Fee Structure (Annual)

CategoryAnnual Fee
Class IX & X (Science & Maths)₹33,600
Class XI & XII (JEE & NEET)₹65,000

Quarterly Payment Plan

The coaching fee is annual in nature. For convenience, it may be paid in four equal quarterly instalments:

QuarterMonths CoveredPayment MonthClass IX & XClass XI & XII
Q1May – JulyMay₹8,400₹16,250
Q2August – OctoberAugust₹8,400₹16,250
Q3November – JanuaryNovember₹8,400₹16,250
Q4February – AprilFebruary₹8,400₹16,250
Total——₹33,600₹65,000

Coaching Schedule

  • Classes IX & X: 3 days a week
  • Classes XI & XII: 6 days a week

Important Instructions

  • The coaching fee is considered annual, irrespective of the payment mode opted.
  • The quarterly payment facility is provided only for the convenience of parents.
  • Parents are encouraged to pay the full annual fee in advance.

General Notes

  • Coaching classes are an additional academic support programme.
  • Admission to coaching classes is subject to availability of seats.
  • Fees once paid are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Regular attendance is expected for optimal academic outcomes.
